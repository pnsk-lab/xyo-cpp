## Compile-time proof

The compiler admits a single nested `control_repeat` in the body of the
selected outer counted loop only if:

- Its TIMES is a **finite integer literal 1..8**, not a variable or dynamic
  expression; nested or cyclic repeat structures are rejected.
- Its inner body has at most four simple numeric variable increments;
  the total statement graph has a budget of 16 visited blocks.
- Every change/set VALUE reporter is from a bounded, acyclic and
  explicit **pure-opcode whitelist** (literal, argument reporter, variable
  read, basic arithmetic or string join). Unknown/extension/async
  expressions are rejected rather than assuming a primitive setter is
  intrinsically synchronous.
- The outer loop retains its native Fiber post-body yield and exact
  interpreter source counter. **Only the approved inner loop's yield
  is suppressed** and opaque GPU/par-for, repeat unroll and similar
  inner optimizations are prohibited for this path.
- The original source-digest proof, JIT pointer lifecycle and existing
  deoptimization machinery from released v1.2.3 remain unchanged.

## Integration

The unrelated shared worktree has more than 1,000 concurrent dirty
entries. It is not used for this release candidate. OSR-011 and later
tests depend on eight additional private JitCtx/CompiledScript ABI
fields and separate RT/LLVM/sequencer logic; they must not be enabled
by this small OSR-010 source extraction.

The v1.2.3 remote main and tag remain unchanged. This is a local
feature integration experiment, not a published release.
