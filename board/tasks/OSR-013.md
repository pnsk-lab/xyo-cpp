## Scope

Experimental, strict opt-in `XYO_JIT_OSR_NESTED_TRIPLE_YIELD=1`, requiring the existing OSR-011/012 guards. Permit exactly two nested counted repeats under the selected outer counted warp repeat. At a *real deepest native Fiber yield*, capture and restore three source loop counters and the original GC-rooted Interpreter caller frames after a real editor-style code change. No arbitrary SSA/effect-PC reconstruction.
